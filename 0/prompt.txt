culprit localise <run_id>
- Splits the culprit commit into hunks, ignoring the test-suite folder.
- Ranks them with one model call, given the diff, the commit's intent (with answer-revealing lines removed) and the failing tasks. If that call fails, it uses diff order.
- Reverts the top 3 hunks one at a time on the culprit commit and re-runs the tasks that distinguished good from bad, then tries pairs, then falls back to "the whole commit".
- Its own budget defaults to $1.50 on top of what the run already spent. Running it again resumes without repeating work.
- On the fake victim, it rejected a wrong top-ranked hunk and found the right one, and it found a case where only reverting two hunks together restores the agent.

culprit fix <run_id>
- The model names what the commit was trying to gain (latency, tokens, cost or none) and returns exact edits, limited to the responsible hunks' files.
- Each fix is checked at the branch head on the full suite plus the held-out tasks, against three gates: quality restored, the intended gain kept (within 10%), and no task that always passed now always fails.
- Failed attempts are fed back to the next one. After 2 failures, it checks the plain revert of the responsible hunks instead.
- On the fake victim, a fix that restored quality but gave the speed-up back was rejected (latency 300 vs 100), and the next attempt was verified.
