# Code of Conduct

Culprit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and welcome people of every background
and experience level. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing someone's
private information are not acceptable, in the repository or in any project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers through
GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form (the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**),
choosing a title that starts with "Conduct:". Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, as described in the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
